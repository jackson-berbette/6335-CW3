Stackbay Warehousing: case setup

What the student data contains so far: one dry warehouse, one simulated year (7 July 2025 to 5 July 2026), pallets in and pallets out.

How a pallet moves

One pallet per slot, one SKU per pallet. A slot empties when its pallet ships and is refilled by a later receipt. The oldest pallet of a SKU ships first.
